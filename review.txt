=== [36] ‘뭐해?’ Greeting ===
(사실 확인용 검색어) Korean informal greeting 뭐해 usage, Korean language casual greeting
(한글 제목) 한국인 인사: 뭐해? 그냥 안부 인사? #Shorts
후크: When a Korean says ‘What are you doing?’, they might just be saying hello.
본문: In casual Korean conversation, especially among friends and younger people, the phrase ‘뭐해?’ (mwohae?) is frequently used as a greeting rather than a literal inquiry, similar to asking “What’s up?” in English. The speaker typically expects a brief response like “nothing” or “just working,” and the exchange serves to acknowledge the other person’s presence. This usage stems from a cultural tendency to start conversations with a light, open‑ended question that can be answered quickly. Because it is informal, it is usually avoided in formal settings or with elders, where a more polite greeting would be chosen.
반전: The same phrase can be heard on public transportation in Seoul, where commuters shout “뭐해?” to start a brief chat before boarding the train.
