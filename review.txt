본문: In South Korea, many families mark the 1,000th day after a child’s birth, called “baek‑il,” with a small gathering of relatives and a special menu that often includes white rice cake (baekseolgi) and seaweed soup. The celebration is less commercial than a first birthday and focuses on family blessings.
