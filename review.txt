후크: On November 11, Koreans swap thin chocolate sticks like love letters.
본문: Pepero Day, observed on 11/11 because the date resembles four stick-shaped snacks, has become a popular occasion for friends, classmates, and coworkers to give each other Pepero, a chocolate‑coated biscuit stick. The tradition started in the 1990s as a marketing event by a confectionery company and spread through schools and offices, where people often write short messages on the sticks. In recent years, many brands have released alternative flavors such as pretzel or almond, and some people use the day to give non‑chocolate gifts as a playful twist. While the day is widely recognized among younger generations, it is not an official holiday and many adults may simply ignore it.
반전: Some retailers report that Pepero sales on 11/11 can exceed the combined sales of the entire month of October.
