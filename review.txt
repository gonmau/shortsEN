=== [83] Reusable Cup Lockers ===
(사실 확인용 검색어) Seoul café reusable cup lockers zero waste
(한글 제목) 한국: 서울 카페, 컵을 잠그다 언제든지 가져가세요
후크: Imagine borrowing a coffee cup, locking it, and never losing it again.
본문: Eco‑focused cafés in neighborhoods like Hongdae and Seongsu have installed small locker banks next to the counter. After you bring your own cup, you scan a QR code, the locker opens, and you store the cup; the system logs the locker number on your phone. When you return, you retrieve it without any exchange of keys, encouraging a zero‑waste habit while keeping your cup safe from being misplaced.
반전: A few of these lockers even charge a tiny fee that donates to local environmental NGOs.
