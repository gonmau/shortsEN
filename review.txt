=== [26] QR‑Only Street Food ===
(사실 확인용 검색어) Korean street food QR payment, cashless street vendors Seoul, KakaoPay tteokbokki
(한글 제목) 한국: QR 전용 길거리 음식 떡볶이 QR로 구매? #Shorts
후크: Ever tried buying tteokbokki with just a QR code?
본문: Since 2020, a growing number of street food stalls in Seoul’s night markets have replaced cash registers with QR‑code payment stickers from apps like KakaoPay and Toss. Customers scan the code on the vendor’s cart, enter the amount, and the payment is confirmed instantly, eliminating the need for change. Vendors say the system speeds up service and reduces the risk of theft, while customers appreciate the convenience of not carrying cash late at night. The trend is most visible around areas like Myeongdong and Hongdae.
반전: Some vendors offer a small discount of about 5 % for QR payments, encouraging the shift away from cash.

후크: Did you know you can rent a mini grill for a picnic right by the river with just a tap?
본문: Along the Han River’s popular parks, automated kiosks allow visitors to rent small charcoal grills, a set of tongs, and a fire‑starter for a nominal fee. After scanning a QR code and paying through a mobile app, the grill is unlocked and can be used at any designated picnic spot for up to two hours. The service was introduced to encourage outdoor cooking while keeping the park clean, as the grills are collected and cleaned by park staff after use. Families and friends often gather to grill samgyeopsal or vegetables while enjoying the night skyline.
반전: If the grill is returned before the time limit, the app refunds the remaining minutes as a discount for the next rental.
