=== [94] Phone Pouch Silence ===
(사실 확인용 검색어) Korean study cafe phone pouch, phone bag silent study Korea, library phone bag etiquette
(한글 제목) 한국: 카페 전화 무음 가방에 전화 숨기기
후크: Ever seen a phone disappear into a clear bag before studying?
본문: Many Korean study cafés and university libraries provide small transparent pouches where patrons place their smartphones at the start of a session. The pouch stays on the table, visually reminding everyone the device is muted, which helps maintain a quiet environment. The practice grew from the high density of students who use phones for both study and distraction, turning a simple bag into a social cue for silence.
반전: Some cafés even lock the pouch with a tiny padlock that only opens when you leave, preventing you from taking the phone back mid‑session.
